# England Health Index (ONS) - subdomains

A dataset containing subdomain health index scores for the English lower
tier local authorities from 2015-2021.

## Usage

``` r
england_health_index_subdomains
```

## Format

A data frame with 2,149 rows and 16 variables:

- ltla21_code:

  2021 Lower tier local authority code

- year:

  Year

- Difficulties in daily life:

  Difficulties in daily life - Health People domain

- Mental health:

  Mental health - Health People domain

- Mortality:

  Mortality - Health People domain

- Personal well-being:

  Personal well-being - Health People domain

- Physical health conditions:

  Physical health conditions - Health People domain

- Behavioural risk factors:

  Behavioural risk factors - Health Lives domain

- Children and young people:

  Children and young people - Health Lives domain

- Physiological risk factors:

  Physiological risk factors - Health Lives domain

- Protective measures:

  Protective measures - Health Lives domain

- Access to green space:

  Access to green space - Health Places domain

- Access to services:

  Access to services - Health Places domain

- Crime:

  Crime - Health Places domain

- Economic and working conditions:

  Economic and working conditions - Health Places domain

- Living conditions:

  Living conditions - Health Places domain
