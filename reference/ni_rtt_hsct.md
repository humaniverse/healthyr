# Consultant-led Referral to Treatment (RTT) by Health & Social Care Trust

A dataset containing RTT data for Health & Social Care Trusts in NI.

## Usage

``` r
ni_rtt_hsct
```

## Format

A data frame with 2,706 rows and 7 variables:

- hsct22_name:

  Health & Social Care Trust name

- date:

  Date

- year:

  Year

- month:

  Month

- specialty:

  Specialty

- waits_over_18_weeks:

  Total waiting \> 18 weeks

- waits_over_52_weeks:

  Total waiting \> 52 weeks

- total_waits:

  Total waits

## Source
